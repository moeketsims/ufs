# Permissions, regulations and logistics

*Researched 3 Oct 2026. Covers the Bloemfontein main campus, South Campus and Qwaqwa campus.*

> **How this was verified.** The research container could not open most primary pages (caa.co.za, ufs.ac.za, acts.co.za were blocked by its network proxy), so claims marked as sourced come from search-engine summaries of the cited pages, not full reads. Items marked **UNVERIFIED** come from general knowledge. Check primary documents before relying on any of this. Nothing here is legal advice.

## Key takeaways

- **Airspace is the biggest constraint.** Both Bloemfontein campuses sit inside the Bloemfontein control zone (CTR), Class C from ground level. The main campus is also within 10 km of New Tempe aerodrome (FATP). Drone work must be done by a licensed commercial operator whose approved manual allows flight in controlled airspace and near aerodromes, with ATC coordination. A hobbyist or student flying privately cannot legally fly there.
- **UFS requires written permission for any commercial filming**, indoors or outdoors, from its Department of Communication and Marketing (DCM) ([UFS guidelines](https://www.ufs.ac.za/media/guidelines/guidelines-for-photography-and-video-recordings)).
- **Plan for disruption.** In October 2025 student protests over registration policy brought classes to a standstill ([Daily Maverick](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2025-10-15-university-of-free-state-students-arrested-as-backlash-over-registration-policy-brings-classes-to-standstill/)). Build contingency days into any schedule.
- **Airfield names:** AFB Bloemspruit shares runways with Bram Fischer International (FABL) ([ACSA](https://www.airports.co.za/business/commercial/business-opportunities/bram-fischer)). FABM is Bethlehem Airport, not Bloemspruit ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bethlehem_Airport)).

## 1. South African drone law (SACAA Part 101)

Part 101 of the Civil Aviation Regulations (first gazetted 2015) separates commercial, corporate, non-profit and private flying ([Lexology](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=c8a92220-c406-483f-af76-19f2df55c465)).

**2023 amendments (latest found).** The 25th and 26th amendments renamed key documents ([amended Part 101, rpaslaunchpad copy](https://www.rpaslaunchpad.com/resources/Part-101-of-25th-and-26th-Amendment-of-the-Civil-Aviation-Regulations-2023.pdf)):

- The operator certificate **ROC** became the **UASOC** (Unmanned Aircraft System Operating Certificate).
- The pilot licence **RPL** became the **RPC** (Remote Pilot Certificate).
- The 26th Amendment is GN R.3170, Government Gazette 48228, 17 March 2023 ([acts.co.za](https://source.acts.co.za/civil-aviation-act-2009/nr425_part_101_subpart_5__ua_operatio.php)).
- SACAA guidance on Part 101 operations manuals is dated 26 Aug 2025 ([SACAA TGM](https://caasanwebsitestorage.blob.core.windows.net/flight-operations-tgm/TGM%20Part%20101%20RPAS%20Operations%20Manual.pdf)).
- No 2024–2026 gazetted amendment was found. **UNVERIFIED** — check SACAA and the Government Gazette.

**A commercial film shoot needs:**

- an RPC/RPL for the pilot;
- a UASOC/ROC for the operator, with an approved operations manual;
- an **Air Services Licence** under the Air Services Licensing Act 115 of 1990 ([filmic.co.za](https://filmic.co.za/permits/));
- an RPAS **Letter of Approval** for the aircraft, valid 12 months;
- aircraft registration and public liability insurance ([audiovisuallab](https://audiovisuallab.co.za/journal/drone-videography-south-africa-corporate/)).

**Distance and altitude rules** (unless SACAA authorises otherwise; [Part 101 amended](https://www.rpaslaunchpad.com/resources/Part-101-of-25th-and-26th-Amendment-of-the-Civil-Aviation-Regulations-2023.pdf), [uavdronesolutions](https://uavdronesolutions.com/blog/drone-laws-south-africa/)):

| Rule | Limit |
|---|---|
| People | Not within 50 m, except crew/participants under the operator's control with safety provisions |
| Buildings and structures | Not within 50 m without the owner's permission (the UFS letter covers this) |
| Public roads | Not over, along or within 50 m unless conditions are met — Nelson Mandela Drive and DF Malherbe Drive border the main campus |
| Altitude | 400 ft (120 m) above ground level |
| Aerodromes | Not within 10 km of an aerodrome reference point |
| Controlled airspace | UASOC holders only, as approved in their operations manual |
| Line of sight (VLOS) | Within 500 m of the pilot |

**Private flying is not an option here.** Private flights are barred from controlled airspace and from within 10 km of aerodromes ([Lexology](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=c8a92220-c406-483f-af76-19f2df55c465)), and film footage for an institution is not private use anyway. Photogrammetry and mapping flights for a digital twin are commercial operations too.

**Students** can fly only with an RPC and under a UASOC holder's certificate (**UNVERIFIED** as a general rule; follows from the above). **Foreign pilots** must pass the local practical test, radiotelephony and English level 4 ([Iris Automation](https://www.irisonboard.com/the-complete-pocket-primer-on-south-africas-drone-laws/)); hiring locally is simpler.

**Cost (indicative):** half day R8,000–R18,000, full day R15,000–R35,000 ([audiovisuallab](https://audiovisuallab.co.za/journal/drone-videography-south-africa-corporate/)); some operators advertise from R4,000 per half day ([Visuals From Above](https://visualsfromabove.co.za/drone-services-pricing)) or R4,500/hour ([Aerial Drone Solutions](https://www.aerialdronesolutions.co.za/drone-photography-videography-pricing)). Expect extra for controlled-airspace paperwork and travel (**UNVERIFIED**).

**Operator leads (not endorsements; no Bloemfontein-based UASOC holder was confirmed):** [THE FUTURE 3D](https://www.thefuture3d.com/locations/south-africa/) (lists Bloemfontein), [Aerial Drone Solutions](https://www.aerialdronesolutions.co.za/), [Flow South Media](https://flowsouthmedia.com/drone-aerial-media-services/), [Epic Air](https://www.epicair.co.za/) (ROC G1336D), [Integrated Aerial Systems](https://iasystems.co.za/) (G1326D), [Unmanned Aerial Solutions](https://www.uaerialsolutions.co.za/) (G1472D), [ABEOD](https://abeod.com/) ("operate under our UASOC"). Ask for the UASOC, Air Services Licence, Letter of Approval and insurance, and check them against SACAA's operator list.

**In-house lead:** UFS's MAGIC drone imaging group ([UFS news, 2022](https://www.ufs.ac.za/templates/news-archive/campus-news/2022/march/ufs-magic-lab-the-go-to-geological-drone-imaging-group-in-south-africa)). Whether it holds a UASOC is **UNVERIFIED**.

## 2. Airspace around the campuses

Straight-line distances from the UFS-published campus GPS points — main −29.107376, 26.187284 ([GPS guide](https://www.ufs.ac.za/docs/default-source/ufs-news-list/gps-gateway-guide.pdf?sfvrsn=d031b721_2)); South −29.181819, 26.216364 ([UFS](https://www.ufs.ac.za/southcampus)); Qwaqwa −28.486241, 28.823281 ([Qwaqwa profile](https://www.ufs.ac.za/docs/default-source/all-documents/2016-qwaqwa-profile.pdf?sfvrsn=0)) — to the FABL reference point ([AIP AD-2 FABL](https://cad.atns.co.za/SmartAIM/EAipPackages/15-JAN-22/2022-01-15-000000/html/eAIP/FA-AD-2-FABL-en-US.html?amdt=show)), FATP ([gcmap](http://www.gcmap.com/airport/FATP)), FABM ([gcmap](http://www.gcmap.com/airport/FABM)) and FAHR ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harrismith_Airport)).

| Campus | To FABL (Bram Fischer + AFB Bloemspruit) | To FATP (New Tempe) | Inside the Bloemfontein CTR? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main (Bloemfontein) | 11.0 km / 6.0 NM | **8.8 km** (≈7.8–9.8 km across campus) | **Yes** |
| South Campus | 12.2 km / 6.6 NM | 17.5 km | **Yes** |
| Qwaqwa | Harrismith (FAHR) 39 km; Bethlehem (FABM) 55 km | — | No nearby aerodrome found (**UNVERIFIED**) |

- The **Bloemfontein CTR** is Class C from ground to 6,500 ft, bounded by 12 NM and 15 NM arcs around FABL (AIP, 2022 edition — check the current one).
- Danger areas: FAD106 (New Tempe Flying Area) lies ≈26 km+ north; FAD107A/B (Bloemfontein Military Flying Areas) exist — overlap with the campuses **UNVERIFIED** ([AIP ENR 5.1](https://cad.atns.co.za/SmartAIM/EAipPackages/15-OCT-21/2021-10-15-000000/html/eAIP/FA-ENR-5.1-en-US.html)).
- **Temporary bans happen** (e.g. a blanket ban over Bloemfontein for Armed Forces Day 2018 — [TimesLIVE](https://www.timeslive.co.za/news/south-africa/2018-02-19-no-fly-zones-for-drones-during-armed-forces-day-celebrations/)). Check NOTAMs for shoot dates.
- **Implication:** the operator coordinates with ATNS Bloemfontein ATC (shared civil/military field) and with FATP for the main campus. Ask them to check hospital helipads near campus (e.g. Universitas Academic Hospital — **UNVERIFIED**). Qwaqwa is probably uncontrolled at low level (**UNVERIFIED**); the usual distance rules still apply.

## 3. UFS institutional rules

From the [UFS guidelines for photography and video recordings](https://www.ufs.ac.za/media/guidelines/guidelines-for-photography-and-video-recordings):

- **Indoor filming always needs DCM permission** — offices, labs, classrooms, residences, eating areas.
- **Commercial or advertising filming needs permission indoors or outdoors.**
- **Request in writing**: project description and intended use, locations, dates, times, duration, number of people and vehicles, and UFS material to be used.
- **Shoots that interfere with academic work or student life will not be approved.**
- **Use of UFS names, names of endearment (e.g. "Kovsies") or buildings** in commercial/entertainment footage needs DCM approval.

**Contacts:** media requests to **news@ufs.ac.za** ([DCM](https://www.ufs.ac.za/advancement/department-of-communication-and-marketing)). Senior Director of Communication and Marketing: Lacea Loader ([IOL, Oct 2025](https://iol.co.za/news/education/2025-10-20-university-of-free-state-resumes-academic-activities-after-violent-protests/)). Phone numbers for media relations and the Qwaqwa contact centre appear on UFS media pages but were not confirmed current (**UNVERIFIED**).

**Venues and events:** external parties follow the UFS events procedure; bookings by email to Facilities Management ([Events Coordinating Procedure](https://www.ufs.ac.za/docs/librariesprovider8/policies-and-guidelines/events-coordination-procedure.pdf?sfvrsn=1554f220_1)).

**Brand:** UFS publishes brand elements and the Vision 130 brand manual (2023); logos come from the intranet. The Vision 130 mark "cannot stand alone". Corporate blue is Pantone 2768C / #0F204B ([Brand Elements](https://www.ufs.ac.za/branding/brand-elements); [Vision 130 manual](https://www.ufs.ac.za/docs/narrativeandbrandguidelineslibraries/brand-guidelines/vision-130-brand-manual---2023.pdf?Status=Master&sfvrsn=690e2c20_3)).

**Drone policy:** no published UFS drone policy found (**UNVERIFIED** that none exists). Treat the DCM letter as the landowner permission Part 101 requires, and copy Protection Services.

**Sport (UNVERIFIED):** Varsity Cup match footage is likely subject to the tournament's broadcast rights. You'd need Varsity Cup management and KovsieSport consent. Shooting empty stadiums or training sessions avoids this.

## 4. People in footage (POPIA) — UNVERIFIED throughout

Primary sources: [POPIA, Act 4 of 2013](https://www.gov.za/documents/protection-personal-information-act); [Information Regulator](https://inforegulator.org.za).

- Identifiable faces and voices are personal information; filming needs a lawful basis (consent or legitimate interest, s11).
- The journalistic/artistic exclusion (s7) is unlikely to cover a promotional or institutional film.
- Children under 18 need parent/guardian consent (ss34–35). Avoid filming minors (e.g. school visitors on open days).
- Image rights also exist outside POPIA: commercial use of someone's likeness without consent infringes identity rights (*Grütter v Lombard*, 2007).
- **Practice:** signed releases for anyone featured or recognisable; "filming in progress" signage with an opt-out; wide or shallow-focus crowd shots; blur objectors; skip clinics, worship spaces and residence rooms; agree storage and retention terms with UFS.
- **AI-generated people** that look like real students raise the same consent and disclosure questions — see the pipeline notes.

## 5. Music — UNVERIFIED

- Commercial SA tracks need a sync licence (publisher; often CAPASSO/SAMRO members) **and** a master-use licence (label). SAMRO/SAMPRA blanket licences cover performance and broadcast, not sync.
- Simpler: a royalty-free library licensed for broadcast, or a commissioned score on full buyout — the UFS Odeion School of Music is an obvious partner.

## 6. Heritage — UNVERIFIED

- Former national monuments became provincial heritage sites under the National Heritage Resources Act 25 of 1999, managed by Heritage Free State.
- A section 27 permit is only needed to alter or damage a site; filming exteriors needs only the owner's (UFS's) permission.
- On set: don't fix rigs, lights or signs to the Main Building; avoid smoke/haze near it; don't land drones on it.

## 7. Safety and logistics

- **Access:** visitors enter at Gate 5, DF Malherbe Drive ([GPS guide](https://www.ufs.ac.za/docs/default-source/ufs-news-list/gps-gateway-guide.pdf?sfvrsn=d031b721_2)). Arrange vehicle access, loading and parking through DCM and Protection Services.
- **Timing:** avoid exam periods and registration week; keep contingency days for protests or closures.
