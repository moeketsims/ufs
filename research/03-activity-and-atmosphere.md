## 2. Scale and daily rhythm

**Enrolment.** "39,000+ students, seven faculties" [S25a]; other UFS material says ~41,000–41,900; the 2024 Annual Performance Plan projects ~42,526 for 2025 [S25d].

| Campus | Students | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Bloemfontein | 26,000+ | [S25b] |
| South Campus | Up to 4,000 contact students; hub for 6,000+ distance learners | [S25e] |
| Qwaqwa | ~3,800 (impact report) **or** 5,400+ (campus page) — conflicting | [S25a][S25c] |

**Residences.** 23 on the Bloemfontein campus; ~3,895 undergraduate beds across Bloemfontein and South Campus; an estimated 20,000–25,000 students live off campus. DBSA-financed residences added 520 beds across Bloemfontein and Qwaqwa (Business Day video, Jul 2026) [S26].

**Daily rhythm.** Lectures Mon–Fri 07:00–19:00 in 50-minute periods with 10-minute changeovers (P1 07:10–08:00, P2 08:10–09:00, …) [S27]. **Foot traffic surges on the hour (≈ :00–:10); paths are near-empty mid-period.** That is the heartbeat a time-lapse or crowd simulation should show.

**Peak spaces.**

- **Thakaneng Bridge** — the student hub: food outlets, ATMs, Van Schaik bookstore, 7-Eleven, copy shops; faces the Sasol Library [S28].
- **Sasol Library** — 24-hour book drop facing the Bridge [S28].
- **Red Square** — modern urban landscaping, water-wise garden, shade trees; used for first-year welcomes [S29].

**Movement.** Netcare-donated minibuses shuttle medical students from the Francois Retief Building to the city's hospitals; Mangaung Transit serves UFS [S30]. An inter-campus shuttle is **UNVERIFIED**.

**Production risk.** Protests disrupted Bloemfontein and Qwaqwa on 12 Feb 2025; Qwaqwa was closed mid-October 2025 [S31].

## 3. Light and climate

### Bloemfontein (~1,400 m, Highveld) [S32]

- January highs ~30.5 °C; June/July highs ~17–18 °C, July nights ~0–2 °C; frost common, snow rare.
- ~580 mm/yr over ~55 rain days, mostly **afternoon thunderstorms** mid-Oct to mid-Apr. Very sunny (~3,300–3,500 h/yr); Oct–Dec sunniest.

| Month | Jan | Feb | Mar | Apr | May | Jun | Jul | Aug | Sep | Oct | Nov | Dec |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rain (mm) | 85 | 110 | 70 | 55 | 15 | 12 | 8 | 15 | 25 | 45 | 60 | 60 |

### Qwaqwa / Phuthaditjhaba — UNVERIFIED (no sourced data retrieved)

~1,650–1,700 m in the Maluti foothills [S38]. Expect cooler temperatures and more summer rain than Bloemfontein (~700–800 mm), hard winter frosts, **snow on the Maluti/Drakensberg peaks** several times Jun–Sep, and towering summer cumulonimbus over the escarpment. Check SAWS records for Harrismith/Witsieshoek.

### Sun times (Bloemfontein campus, SAST)

From `data/sun-times.csv` (all three campuses, 1st and 15th of each month, Nov 2026 – Dec 2027). Golden hour is astral's definition (sun between −4° and +6°); blue hour is −6° to −4°.

| Date | Blue hour AM | Sunrise | Golden hour AM | Solar noon (sun elevation) | Golden hour PM | Sunset | Blue hour PM |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2027-02-15 | 05:31–05:40 | 05:56 | 05:40–06:27 | 12:29 (73.6°) | 18:30–19:17 | 19:01 | 19:17–19:26 |
| 2027-05-15 | 06:25–06:35 | 06:51 | 06:35–07:24 | 12:11 (42.0°) | 16:58–17:47 | 17:31 | 17:47–17:57 |
| 2027-08-15 | 06:23–06:32 | 06:48 | 06:32–07:20 | 12:19 (46.9°) | 17:19–18:07 | 17:51 | 18:07–18:16 |
| 2027-11-15 | 04:46–04:56 | 05:13 | 04:56–05:45 | 11:59 (79.4°) | 18:14–19:03 | 18:46 | 19:03–19:13 |

**Sun-angle notes.** The noon sun stands in the **north**. In winter it rises ~63° (ENE) and sets ~297° (WNW): low, warm light rakes north-facing facades all day while south facades stay in shade; usable "long light" from ~15:30. In summer it rises ~117° (ESE) and sets ~243° (WSW); midday light at 79–84° is flat and harsh, so shoot early or late. Qwaqwa runs ~10–12 minutes earlier than Bloemfontein, and the mountains may cut its light short (**UNVERIFIED**, depends on bearing).

### Seasonal cues

- **City of Roses:** Bloemfontein Rose Festival every **October**, when roses peak (since 1976) [S33]. **MACUFE** runs ~10 days from early October [S33].
- **Jacarandas** bloom Sep–Nov in Gauteng, increasingly early [S34]; jacarandas on the UFS campus are **not documented** — scout in Sep–Oct.
- Autumn colour (Apr–May), frosted lawns (Jun–Jul) and bleached winter veld with dust (Aug–Sep) are typical of the Highveld (**UNVERIFIED** for campus specifically).

## 5. Reference projects — UNVERIFIED (background knowledge)

- **West Cambridge digital twin** (Centre for Digital Built Britain, ~2019–22): building model linked to live sensors; effective because operations data sits on the 3D model.
- **Cesium for Unreal + Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles:** seamless zoom from globe to street; quality over Bloemfontein unknown and licence limits apply (see `02-geospatial-data.md`).
- **Esri ArcGIS smart-campus twins:** occupancy and energy storytelling tied to geography.
- **SA university films** (Stellenbosch, UCT, Wits, UP): the strongest share a dawn-to-night arc, people-first rituals (serenades, graduations) and a landscape anchor (Table Mountain, Jonkershoek). UFS equivalents: **Naval Hill views over Bloemfontein** and the **Maluti escarpment behind Qwaqwa**.
